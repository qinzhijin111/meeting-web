
# AgentMeet Demo Privacy Notice

## AI and Transcription

AgentMeet does not silently start AI transcription.

The meeting host must explicitly start the Note Taker Agent.
When the Note Taker is active, participants see a visible notice
that AI transcription and processing are taking place.

## Data Processed

When the Note Taker is active, participant microphone audio is
processed in real time for speech-to-text transcription.

The MVP does not intentionally store raw meeting audio.

Final transcript information may be processed to generate:

- a meeting summary
- decisions
- action items
- open questions

## Stored Meeting Output

Structured meeting notes are stored in the application database
and are associated with the meeting room and Note Taker session.

The current MVP allows the room host to view the post-meeting
summary by default.

## AI Accuracy

Meeting notes are AI-generated and may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Users should verify important decisions, assignments, deadlines,
and other meeting information before relying on the generated notes.

## Demo Use Only

AgentMeet is an internship demonstration project.

It should not be used for confidential customer meetings,
medical information, regulated data, or other sensitive production
workloads.
